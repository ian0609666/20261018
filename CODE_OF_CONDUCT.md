# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or
level of technical knowledge.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give constructive feedback about the work, not the person.
- Accept corrections gracefully and help others learn.
- Protect private information and follow responsible disclosure practices.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, publication of
private information, or sustained disruption of project spaces is not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub
profile contact options. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect
the community. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
